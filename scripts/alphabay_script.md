[VISUAL] A new logo fades in, glowing: ALPHABAY.

## Clip 4 — Building the Empire

[VISUAL] AlphaBay homepage loads. Clean, professional, search bar, categories.

[VISUAL] Feature icons pop in: lock, shield, two-factor, wallet.
Cazes built in better security than anyone before him. Encrypted messages. Two-factor logins. A system where buyers' money was held safely until the order arrived.
[VISUAL] Staff blobs appear around Alpha02: moderators, support, security.
He hired staff. Moderators. Customer support. A security team. He wasn't a lone hacker in a bedroom. He was a CEO.
[VISUAL] Category list scrolls: Drugs, Fraud, Hacking Tools, Stolen Data, Weapons.
And unlike Silk Road, AlphaBay sold almost everything. Drugs, yes. But also stolen credit cards, hacked accounts, malware, fake IDs.
[VISUAL] Competitor logos fall away behind AlphaBay. It grows bigger and bigger.
As the other markets collapsed or scammed their users, people flooded in. Within two years... AlphaBay was the biggest black market on the planet.
